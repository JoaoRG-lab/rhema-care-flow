 # RheumaFlow Contributor Verification
 
 **Version 1.0** | Credentialing Standards for Clinical Contributors
 
 ---
 
 ## Overview
 
 RheumaFlow maintains high standards for clinical content quality by verifying the credentials of healthcare professionals who contribute insights, review disease activity scores, and edit clinical guidelines. This document outlines our verification requirements and process.
 
 ---
 
 ## Verification Categories
 
 ### 1. University Affiliation
 
 Contributors must demonstrate active affiliation with an accredited academic institution.
 
 **Accepted Documentation:**
 - Institutional email address verification (@edu, @ac.uk, etc.)
 - Faculty appointment letter or contract
 - Department roster or directory listing
 - Academic ID card with current validity
 
 **Verification Levels:**
 | Level | Description | Requirements |
 |-------|-------------|--------------|
 | Resident/Fellow | Trainee in rheumatology program | Program director attestation |
 | Faculty | Academic appointment | Appointment letter, institutional profile |
 | Department Head | Division/section leadership | Official position documentation |
 | Emeritus | Retired faculty with continued engagement | Emeritus status confirmation |
 
 ---
 
 ### 2. Board Certification
 
 Rheumatology board certification validates specialized training and competency.
 
 **Accepted Certifications:**
 
 | Region | Certifying Body | Credential |
 |--------|-----------------|------------|
 | USA | American Board of Internal Medicine | Rheumatology Subspecialty |
 | UK | Royal College of Physicians | CCT in Rheumatology |
 | EU | UEMS Section of Rheumatology | European Board Examination |
 | Canada | Royal College of Physicians and Surgeons | FRCPC Rheumatology |
 | Brazil | Sociedade Brasileira de Reumatologia | Título de Especialista |
 | International | Various national boards | Equivalent certification |
 
 **Required Documentation:**
 - Board certificate copy (with validity dates)
 - Certification verification letter
 - MOC/Recertification status (if applicable)
 
 ---
 
 ### 3. Medical License
 
 Active medical licensure confirms legal authorization to practice.
 
 **Verification Requirements:**
 - Active, unrestricted medical license
 - License number and issuing authority
 - Verification through primary source (where available)
 - No current disciplinary actions
 
 **License Status Categories:**
 | Status | Eligibility |
 |--------|-------------|
 | Active - Unrestricted | ✅ Full contributor access |
 | Active - Restricted | ⚠️ Case-by-case review |
 | Inactive/Retired | ✅ With board certification maintained |
 | Suspended/Revoked | ❌ Not eligible |
 
 ---
 
 ### 4. Publications & Research
 
 Scholarly contributions demonstrate expertise and credibility.
 
 **Recognized Contributions:**
 
 - **Peer-reviewed Publications**
   - First/last author on rheumatology journal articles
   - Contribution to clinical practice guidelines (ACR, EULAR, OARSI)
   - Systematic reviews and meta-analyses
 
 - **Clinical Research**
   - Principal investigator on rheumatology clinical trials
   - Co-investigator with significant protocol contribution
   - Translational research publications
 
 - **Educational Content**
   - Textbook chapters in rheumatology references
   - CME/CPD course development
   - Invited lectures at major conferences (ACR, EULAR)
 
 **Verification Methods:**
 - ORCID profile with linked publications
 - PubMed author search verification
 - ClinicalTrials.gov investigator listing
 - Conference abstract archives

 ## Contributor Privileges
 
 Verified contributors gain access to enhanced platform capabilities:
 
 ### Add Clinical Insights
 
 Verified rheumatologists can contribute:
 - Clinical pearls and practice tips
 - Medication management recommendations
 - Differential diagnosis guidance
 - Patient education materials
 
 ### Review Scores
 
 Participate in clinical scoring oversight:
 - Validate disease activity score calculations
 - Provide interpretation context
 - Flag potential calculation errors
 - Suggest score threshold adjustments based on evidence
 
 ### Edit Guidelines
 
 Contribute to protocol development:
 - Propose updates to monitoring protocols
 - Suggest treatment algorithm modifications
 - Update safety monitoring checklists
 - Incorporate new guideline recommendations

 ## Verification Process
 
 ### Step 1: Application
 
 Submit verification request with:
 - Professional profile information
 - Documentation for each credential category
 - ORCID identifier (if available)
 - Brief statement of expertise areas
 
 ### Step 2: Review
 
 Our verification team will:
 - Validate submitted documentation
 - Perform primary source verification where possible
 - Cross-reference public databases (NPI, GMC, AMB, etc.)
 - Complete review within 5-7 business days
 
 ### Step 3: Approval
 
 Upon verification:
 - Receive verified contributor badge
 - Access tier-appropriate privileges
 - Annual re-verification reminder
 - Opt-in to contributor directory
 
 ---
 
 ## Maintaining Verification
 
 ### Annual Requirements
 
 - Confirm continued license validity
 - Update board certification status
 - Attest to no disciplinary actions
 - Review and accept contributor guidelines
 
 ### Credential Changes
 
 Notify RheumaFlow within 30 days of:
 - License suspension or restriction
 - Board certification lapse
 - Institutional affiliation changes
 - Name or credential updates
 
 ---
 
 ## Ethical Standards
 
 Verified contributors agree to:
 
 1. **Accuracy**: Provide evidence-based information only
 2. **Disclosure**: Declare relevant conflicts of interest
 3. **Privacy**: Maintain patient de-identification standards
 4. **Respect**: Engage constructively with other contributors
 5. **Currency**: Base recommendations on current guidelines
 
 ---
 
 ## Appeals Process
 
 If verification is denied:
 
 1. Review denial reason provided
 2. Submit additional documentation within 30 days
 3. Request secondary review by committee
 4. Final decision within 14 days of appeal
